About

Hey — I’m Zeinab.

Zeinab Drameh is a designer, developer, and strategist at the USC Iovine and Young Academy. Her work spans UX, brand, data, machine learning, nonprofit communications, and research.

Rooted in Central LA, she builds at the intersection of design, technology, and social impact — bringing both creative and analytical thinking to every brief.

In her free time: reading, friends, post-punk, and making art.
