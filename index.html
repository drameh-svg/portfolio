The Shade Gap

Interactive insights into LA's shade-inequality problem. With intuitive and insightful data visualizations, policy proposals, strategy in anticipation of the 2028 olympics, and "cool roof" solutions.

Overview

For this project we researched the current problem area, identifying key issues and historical practices like redlining which have exasterbated this issue. To communicate the problem more effectively, we used data visualization techniques to map canopy coverage against heat levels, revealing clearly how poorer neighborhoods receive less shade and as a byproduct, experience higher temperatures and worse health conditions. Following the research, we looked for solutions, drawing heavily from Shade-LA. We came up with an easy to implement change to tree spacing guidelines, as well as drawing inspiration from New York's "cool roof" solution. We also researched next-steps and ways to realistically implement these ideas.

Eth-X

Responsible AI Browser Extension exposing biases and addressing accessibility gaps for AI tools.

Overview

Eth-X is a browser extension and website that exposes biases in AI while also providing tools for underprivileged communities to learn about and use AI. It detects bias using a self-made adversarial test set, breaks down complex AI policies through a summarized terms and conditions page for popular AI brands, and provides interactive resources like news articles and videos to keep users informed about recent news in the field and to bridge the AI literacy gap. Users can also file claims to report misuse or request to add a new company.

2026 ML & Data Analytics Arkadium × IYA

Mapping LLM Limits Through Gameplay

With Arkadium and Iovine and Young Academy, evaluating whether frontier LLMs can reason under structured, rule-bound conditions.

Overview

We asked a simple question: can frontier models actually follow rules when the world is a game? I helped design and run an evaluation of Claude Opus, Gemini, and Grok across 5 Arkadium games and 115 episodes, scoring every turn in real time as a rule violation, a legal but sub-optimal move, or a strategic one. Models were strong at logical deduction — 94.3% competency on Ancestral Lines — and weak wherever spatial awareness mattered. Play also got worse as sessions ran longer, and 18.1% of all moves were illegal, including nearly a third of Grok's. Gameplay here is a stress test for constraint-following, not just a leaderboard.

2026 ML & Data Analytics Image Classification

Pneumonia Detection

Chest X-ray classification comparing a CNN, SVM, and Random Forest as a clinical decision-support study.

Overview

This project asks whether machine learning can reliably distinguish pneumonia from healthy lungs in chest X-rays — and whether those predictions can help clinicians screen faster. I trained and compared three models on ~5,800 grayscale X-rays: a convolutional neural network, an RBF support vector machine, and a random forest. Random Forest led on this data scale (82.4% test accuracy, AUC ~0.98), with the CNN close behind at 80%. The models are framed as a second-opinion tool, not a replacement for clinical judgment. Full code lives in the pneumonia_classification folder of my ML projects repo.
